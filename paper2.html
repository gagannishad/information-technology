  color: red;
  font-family: "Comic Sans MS";
}
</style>
</head>
<body>
  <h1>Company Name</h1>
</body>
</html>
2. Form to accept name, age, email address with a submit button.
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<body>
<form>
  Name: <input type="text" name="n"><br>
  Age: <input type="number" name="a"><br>
  Email: <input type="email" name="e"><br>
  <input type="submit" value="Submit">
</form>
</body>
</html>
3. Create ordered list with names of tourist Cities, unordered list with tourist places, and divide list into left and right using CSS.
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>
<style>
  .left { float: left; width: 50%; }
  .right { float: right; width: 50%; }
</style>
</head>
<body>
  <div class="left">
    <h3>Tourist Cities</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Pune</li>
      <li>Delhi</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <div class="right">
    <h3>Tourist Places</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Shanivarwada</li>
      <li>Red Fort</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</body>
</html>
From Chapter 2 (Concepts)
4. What is Digital Marketing?
Digital marketing is the use of the internet, mobile devices, social media, search engines, and other channels to reach consumers.
5. Explain White Hat SEO.
White hat SEO involves looking for ways to improve user experience ethically and genuinely, creating content for the users and not just for search engines.
6. Explain the term 'Bounce Rate'.
Bounce rate calculates how many visitors hit the back button or closed your website without performing a single interaction.
From Chapter 4 (Concepts)
7. Explain phases of trade cycle.
A general trade cycle consists of 4 phases: Pre-Sales (Search, Negotiate), Execution (Order, Delivery), Settlement (Invoice, Payment), and After-Sales (Warranty, Maintenance).
8. Describe process of encryption.
Encryption converts Plain text (readable form) into Cipher Text (coded form) to protect information sent over the internet. Decryption is the opposite process. It has Symmetric and Asymmetric types.
9. Which are the different types of e-governance?
The 4 types of E-Governance are: G2C (Government-to-Citizen), G2B (Government-to-Business), G2G (Government-to-Government), and G2E (Government-to-Employee).
