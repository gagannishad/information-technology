Questions & Answers
Chapter 1
1. Write a program using html with CSS: background green, text red, comic sans ms font.
<style>
h1 {
  background-color: green;
  color: red;
  font-family: "Comic Sans MS";
}
</style>
2. What is the use of the <meta> tag in HTML5?
The <meta> tag provides metadata about the document like author, description, and keywords, which are heavily used by search engines for ranking.
Chapter 2
3. What is Digital Marketing?
Digital marketing is the use of the internet, mobile devices, social media, search engines, and other digital channels to reach consumers.
4. What are the three stages of the purchasing funnel?
1) Acquisition (creating interest), 2) Behavior (user engagement), and 3) Conversion (the user becomes a paying customer).
Chapter 4
5. What is E-commerce?
E-Commerce is defined as the process of buying and selling of goods or services using an electronic medium such as the Internet.
6. Explain the phases of a trade cycle.
A general trade cycle consists of 4 phases: 1) Pre-Sales (Search and Negotiate), 2) Execution (Order and Delivery), 3) Settlement (Invoice and Payment), and 4) After-Sales (Warranty and Maintenance).
