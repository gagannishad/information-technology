Q.6 Answer in brief (Textbook Questions)

1. Explain phases of trade cycle.
Ans: A general trade cycle consists of four phases: 1) Pre-Sales (Search and Negotiate), 2) Execution (Order and Delivery), 3) Settlement (Invoice and Payment), and 4) After-Sales (Warranty and Maintenance).
2. Explain M-Commerce.
Ans: Mobile commerce (M-Commerce) is the buying and selling of goods and services through wireless handheld devices such as smartphones and tablets. Applications include mobile banking, ticket booking, and e-bill payments.
3. Describe process of encryption.
Ans: Encryption converts Plain text (readable form of data) into Cipher Text (coded, non-readable form) to protect sensitive information being sent over the internet. Decryption is the opposite process. It has two types: Symmetric (Private-Key) and Asymmetric (Public-Key).
4. What is E-governance.
Ans: E-governance is the implementation of information technology in the government processes and functions to deliver SMART (Simple, Moral, Accessible, Responsive, Transparent) government services to citizens and businesses.
5. List out advantages of E-commerce.
Ans: Advantages include: Global scope, Electronic transactions, Cost Saving, Anytime shopping (24x7), No intermediaries, and better delivery of Public services.
6. Which are the different types of e-governance?
Ans: The four types are: G2C (Government-to-Citizen), G2B (Government-to-Business), G2G (Government-to-Government), and G2E (Government-to-Employee).
7. State two examples of G2E services.
Ans: Applying for leave online, reviewing salary payment records, and checking the balance of holiday.
8. Write any four advantages of e-governance.
Ans: Four advantages are: 1) Reduced corruption, 2) High transparency, 3) Increased convenience, and 4) Reduction in overall cost.

Q.7 Answer the Following (Any 5) [10 Marks]

1. What is E-commerce?
Ans: E-Commerce can be broadly defined as the process of buying and selling of goods or services using an electronic medium such as the Internet. It is a paperless exchange of business information.
2. List advantages of E-commerce.
Ans: Advantages include Global scope, Electronic transactions (less paperwork), Cost Saving, Anytime shopping (24x7), No intermediaries (direct communication), and efficient delivery of Public services.
3. Explain C2C and C2B models.
Ans: C2C (Consumer to Consumer): Consumers sell their assets (e.g., used cars, renting rooms) to other consumers via a website (e.g., OLX). C2B (Consumer to Business): Consumers provide products or services of value that can be consumed by businesses (e.g., writing a blog for a business).
4. What is EDI?
Ans: Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) is the electronic interchange of business information using a standardized format. It allows computers of different companies to securely exchange documents like purchase orders and invoices without paper.
5. Explain the Settlement phase of the Trade Cycle.
Ans: The Settlement phase consists of two steps: Invoice and Payment. The customer receives a bill (invoice) for the purchased product, and after confirming the received product, the customer pays for it electronically.
6. What is E-governance and state its types?
Ans: E-Governance is the implementation of information technology in government processes to create a Simple, Moral, Accessible, Responsive, and Transparent (SMART) government. Its 4 types are G2C, G2B, G2G, and G2E.
7. Describe the process of Encryption and Decryption.
Ans: Encryption: Converts plain text (readable data) into cipher text (coded, non-readable data) to protect it during internet transmission. Decryption: The exact opposite process, converting cipher text back into readable plain text at the destination.
