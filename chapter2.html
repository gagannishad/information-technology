Q.7 Answer the Following (Any 5) [10 Marks]

1. What is Digital Marketing?
Ans: Digital marketing is the use of the internet, mobile devices, social media, search engines, and other digital channels to reach consumers. It is similar to traditional advertising but utilizes digital devices.
2. Explain White Hat SEO.
Ans: White hat SEO involves looking for ways to improve user experience ethically and genuinely. It ensures that web page content is created for the users and not just to manipulate search engines.
3. Explain the term 'Bounce Rate'.
Ans: Bounce rate is a metric in Google Analytics that calculates how many visitors hit the back button or close the website without performing a single interaction (like clicking a link) on the page.
4. What are the three stages of the purchasing funnel?
Ans: The purchasing funnel consists of three stages: 1) Acquisition (creating interest), 2) Behavior (watching videos, clicking descriptions), and 3) Conversion (the user actually becomes a customer).
5. Name any four Digital Marketing channels.
Ans: Four channels include: 1) Search Engine Optimization (SEO), 2) Email Marketing, 3) Content Marketing, and 4) Social Media Marketing.
6. Differentiate between Long tail and Short tail keywords.
Ans: A short tail keyword is broad and highly competitive (e.g., "shoes"). A long tail keyword is a longer, more specific phrase (e.g., "Brown Soft Comfortable Running shoes"), which has less competition and helps rank higher.
7. What is Technical SEO?
Ans: Technical SEO is the process of optimizing backend website elements so search engines can crawl and index them effectively. Examples include improving page load speed and ensuring mobile-friendliness.
