Q.7 Answer the Following - Any 5 (10 Marks)
1. What is E-commerce?
E-Commerce can be broadly defined as the process of buying and selling of goods or services using an electronic medium such as the Internet. It functions as a paperless exchange of business information.
2. What is Digital Marketing?
Digital marketing involves using digital devices, the internet, social media, search engines, and other channels to reach consumers. It acts similarly to traditional advertising but specifically targets audiences online where they spend most of their time.
3. Explain any two types of CSS styling methods.
1) Inline CSS: Used to apply styling to a single specific line or element using the 'style' attribute.

2) Internal (Embedded) CSS: Written inside the <style> tag within the <head> section to format an entire single document or page.
4. Describe the process of Encryption.
Encryption is the process used to protect sensitive user information sent over the internet. It converts Plain Text (readable form of data) into Cipher Text (coded, non-readable form). This ensures that intercepted data cannot be understood by unauthorized third parties.
5. Differentiate between Long tail and Short tail keywords.
A Short tail keyword is brief, broad, and highly competitive (e.g., "shoes"), making it hard to rank top on search engines. A Long tail keyword is a more specific phrase (e.g., "Brown Soft Comfortable Running shoes"), which has less competition and targets a very specific audience, helping rank the website higher.
